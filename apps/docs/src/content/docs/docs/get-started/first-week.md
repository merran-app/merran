title: Your first week
description: Take a recipe through the plan, shopping list, pantry, and cook view.
---

Start with one meal. You can try the core workflow without configuring AI or connecting a store.

## 1. Add a recipe

Open **Recipes** and add a recipe manually or import a recipe URL. Review the imported ingredients, servings, and steps before relying on them. A site that blocks automated requests may need another import method.

For all import methods, editing, units, and migration, see [Recipes](../../use/recipes/).

## 2. Put it on the plan

Open **Plan**, choose a day and meal, and add the recipe. Adjust the servings for your household. A small plan is enough to try the workflow; you do not need to fill every meal.

See [Planning](../../use/planning/) for servings, theme nights, and filling the week without AI.

## 3. Check the pantry

Add the food you already have to **Pantry**, including amounts and units where available. Accurate quantities help Merran work out what still needs buying.

## 4. Build the shopping list

In **List**, add ingredients from the plan. Review the combined quantities and pantry deductions. You can add household items by hand and check off purchases while shopping.

A store connection is optional. Try the list on its own before configuring prices or cart handoff.

See [Shopping and pantry](../../use/shopping-and-pantry/) for quantity matching, purchases, and optional store connections.

## 5. Cook

Open the recipe's cook view and follow the steps. Try the timers and serving controls. Voice and AI features may need additional setup; the recipe and steps remain useful on their own.

See [Cooking](../../use/cooking/) for checklists, timers, voice requirements, and notes.

## Make it your own

Once the loop works, add more recipes, plan a few dinners, and configure the optional modules you want in [settings](../../run/configuration/).
