## Every week

Things you buy each week regardless of the meals planned, like bananas. They join the list when you add the week’s plan.

## Always on hand

Staples you assume are available, such as salt. Merran leaves them off the shopping list.

## Use soon

Pantry food approaching its best-before date. Check the food itself before cooking.

## The week’s pool

Meals picked for this week without a particular day yet. Move them onto a day when you decide.

## Theme nights

A theme for each dinner, such as taco night. Fill the week uses these themes when suggesting recipes.

## Goes with

Recipes you pair together, such as a main dish and a side.

## Proposals and Review

Changes suggested by the assistant are proposals. Review them and choose what to apply.

## Whose AI key pays?

Households can use their own provider keys. Server keys are shared only under the administrator’s policy; provider charges go to the key’s account.
