Merran shows you the language your browser asks for when it has it, and English when it doesn't. You can choose a language of your own instead.

## Choose a language

1. Open **Settings → You**.
2. Under **Language**, choose your language. Each one is listed by its own name.

**Expected result:** Merran switches at once, without reloading. Your choice is saved with your account, so it follows you to every device you sign in on. Anything not translated yet shows in English.

To go back to your browser's language, choose **Match my browser**.

The **Language** setting appears once Merran has more than one language. Before you sign in, Merran uses your browser's language.
